# AI Agent for AU Strategy Implementation
## Project Overview
This project, an original concept from my PhD research on AI’s influence in Africa, operationalizes the African Union (AU) Continental AI Strategy by deploying AI-powered intelligent agents. Inspired by the AU’s strategy, I recognized that agents can rapidly collect and analyze AI adoption patterns across all 54 AU member states, automate critical tasks, and raise awareness to accelerate strategy implementation. Built in just 4–5 hours using Microsoft Copilot’s consumer insights template, akin to managed cloud services, this solution simplifies development for non-technical users while delivering high-impact results. The agents currently:
Populate a prototype AI transparency register.

Monitor strategy implementation progress.

Compile a database of public sector and sector-specific AI use cases (e.g., agriculture, education) using publicly available online data.

# Purpose
The intelligent agents, designed as augmentative tools to empower users rather than replace jobs, aim to:
Automate Data Processes: Streamline data collection and analysis of AI adoption, reducing manual effort by up to 50% and aligning with the Continental AI Strategy and AI Talent Readiness Index.

Enhance Transparency: Develop tools to automatically populate prototype AI transparency registers and compile a database targeting 100+ public sector and sector-specific AI use cases (e.g., agriculture, education), raising awareness of AI’s potential across Africa’s low-access regions. Future integration of Copilot’s data analyst template will enable dynamic visualizations to further support stakeholder engagement.

Support Policymaking: Provide a prototype online portal for researchers and policymakers to access agent-compiled data and, once visualization is implemented, interactive insights tailored to Africa’s diverse regions.

Promote Responsible AI: Incorporate fairness, transparency, and bias mitigation, ensuring ethical implementation that prioritizes African contexts and addresses job displacement concerns.

Provide Policy Insights: Deliver 5–10 data-driven policy recommendations to the AU and member states, leveraging agent outputs and future visualizations to drive strategy operationalization.



While visualization is not yet supported, the agents will be enhanced by integrating Copilot’s data analyst template to enable dynamic visualizations (e.g., heatmaps, trend charts) of AI adoption trends. As a non-profit venture, this project showcases AI’s transformative potential in Africa’s low-access regions through accessible, augmentative tools integrated into a prototype online portal for policymakers, researchers, and communities.

# Starter Prompts
To guide the research and development process, the following prompts are used:
AI Adoption: “What are the current patterns of AI adoption in African public sector organizations and industries like agriculture and healthcare, based on publicly available data?”

Strategic Alignment: “How do AI adoption trends in Africa align with the strategic priorities of the Continental AI Strategy and the AI Talent Readiness Index?”

Transparency Registers: “What progress has been made in establishing AI transparency registers in African countries, and what online data sources can support automation?”

Use Cases: “What are examples of successful AI use cases in African public sectors, agriculture, and education, extracted from publicly available sources?”

Visualization Enhancement: “How can Microsoft Copilot’s data analyst template be integrated to visualize AI adoption trends (e.g., heatmaps, sector-specific charts) for the AU Continental AI Strategy?”

Challenges: “What are the key barriers to implementing the AU Continental AI Strategy in Africa’s low-access regions, and how can accessible, visualization-enabled agents address these?”

# General Guidelines
Request clarification if the prompt lacks a specific AI initiative or country.

Provide accurate, relevant insights on AI adoption, usage, and strategic alignment, emphasizing accessibility for non-technical users.

Acknowledge the current lack of visualization but highlight plans to integrate the data analyst template for enhanced functionality.

Ensure technical accuracy in describing agent functionalities, emphasizing their augmentative role in supporting human efforts.

Maintain a professional, friendly tone and avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information.

Ask engaging follow-on questions to understand the user’s goals (e.g., raising AI awareness, collaborating with stakeholders, or refining visualization features).

# Technical Implementation
The project leverages Microsoft technologies and the Copilot consumer insights template, akin to managed cloud services, to enable rapid, accessible development for non-technical users. Key components include:
Current Architecture:
Microsoft Copilot Studio (Consumer Insights Template): Used to develop the agents in 4–5 hours, providing pre-built functionalities for data extraction and task automation, similar to managed cloud services that abstract complexity.

Azure AI: Powers natural language processing (NLP) and web scraping to extract AI use case data from unstructured online sources (e.g., news articles, government reports).

Power Apps: Hosts the prototype online portal, displaying agent outputs like transparency register entries and use case databases (Future)

Human-in-the-Loop: Enables manual validation of agent outputs to ensure accuracy and adherence to Responsible AI principles (e.g., fairness, transparency). (Future)

# Planned Visualization Enhancement:
Copilot Data Analyst Template: Will be integrated to process agent outputs and generate dynamic visualizations (e.g., heatmaps of AI adoption, trend charts for sectors like education). This template, like a managed service, provides pre-configured data processing and visualization tools, reducing development effort.

Power BI Integration: Will connect with Power Apps to display visualizations in the portal, enabling interactive insights for policymakers.

# Responsible AI Practices:
Bias Mitigation: Agents avoid over-representation of certain regions or sectors, ensuring equitable data collection.

Transparency: Outputs include metadata (e.g., data sources) to maintain accountability.

Security: Data pipelines use encryption and adhere to AU ethical guidelines.

# Development Context:
Built in 4–5 hours using the consumer insights template, proving that non-technical users can create impactful AI solutions with minimal effort, similar to leveraging managed cloud services for rapid deployment.
